Speech to text

Whisper speech recognition, running on your device. Click a timestamp to play from there; click text to fix a word.

Transcribes the selected part of your voice note. Choose the language that is spoken in the recording; the text comes out in that language unless you tick Translate to English. The model downloads once, then works offline.

Effects rack

Signal flows top to bottom. Moving a slider switches its module on. Double-click a slider to reset it.

Opens most voice note formats your browser can decode: WhatsApp and Telegram .opus/.ogg, iPhone .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .webm. Audio is processed at 48 kHz and exported as 16-bit WAV.

Speech to text uses OpenAI's open-source Whisper models through transformers.js (Hugging Face). The model files download from huggingface.co on first use and are cached by your browser. If this view blocks the download, open the standalone voice-studio/index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

AI voice isolation uses the open-source RNNoise model (Xiph.Org) in the @jitsi/rnnoise-wasm build, downloaded once from jsDelivr (1.9 MB) when you switch it on. If your browser blocks it, the spectral noise reduction module still works offline.
